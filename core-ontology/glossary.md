# Glossary

This glossary defines terms as used within the augmented-agency and IAM corpus.

## Agent
A system that executes actions autonomously or semi-autonomously on behalf of a user.

## Agency
The human capacity to form, revise, and sustain intention across time.

## Continuity
The structural condition that enables re-entry into prior activity without reconstruction.

## Coherence
Local consistency within a bounded interaction.

## IAM
Intelligent Augmented Memory.  
An application and interface that supports imagination, attention, and meaning by preserving continuity.

## LUI (Language–User Input)
Explicit human-generated language acts captured without semantic inference.

## Re-entry
The act of returning to a prior region of activity with orientation intact.

## Return Point
A structural marker that enables re-entry into continuity.

## Substrate
A non-semantic structural layer that preserves continuity.

## Trace
A numeric or structural record generated by activity, without encoded meaning.

## Manifold
A structured space in which continuity relationships are maintained.
