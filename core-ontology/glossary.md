# Glossary

This glossary defines terms as used within the augmented-agency and IAM corpus.

## Agent
A system that executes actions autonomously or semi-autonomously on behalf of a user.

## Agency
The human capacity to form, revise, and sustain intention across time.

## Continuity
The structural condition that enables re-entry into prior activity without reconstruction.

## Coherence
Local consistency within a bounded interaction.

## IAM
Intelligent Augmented Memory.  
An application and interface that supports imagination, attention, and meaning by preserving continuity.

## LUI (Language–User Input)
An explicit language act captured as an event of record, without semantic inference.  
LUIs are preserved as opaque traces with stable identifiers, ordering, and provenance.

## Atlas (Continuity Atlas)
The persisted continuity substrate that stores LUI events and continuity structures  
(e.g., ordering, regions, links, return points).

The Atlas is a data substrate. It does not interpret meaning.

## CAP (Continuity Access Protocol)
The interface and protocol by which continuity structures in the Atlas are accessed, navigated, and referenced.

CAP defines permitted operations over Atlas identifiers while enforcing continuity and privacy invariants.  
CAP is not the substrate. CAP is not an agent. CAP does not infer meaning.

## Re-entry
The act of returning to a prior region of activity with orientation intact.

## Return Point
A structural marker that enables re-entry into continuity.

## Substrate
A non-semantic structural layer that preserves continuity independent of interpretation.

## Trace
A numeric or structural record generated by activity, without encoded meaning.

## Manifold
A structured space in which continuity relationships are maintained.
