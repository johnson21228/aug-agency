# Invariants

This document defines **non-negotiable invariants** of the system architecture.

These invariants must hold across all implementations.

## Non-Semantic Invariant

The continuity substrate must not:
- Encode meaning
- Infer intent
- Model beliefs
- Interpret language
- Optimize outcomes

Semantics belong exclusively to the human.

## Explicit Capture Invariant

All human inputs must be:
- Explicitly generated
- Explicitly captured
- Explicitly attributable to a moment of expression

No inferred state may substitute for explicit input.

## Re-entry Invariant

Continuity must support:
- Return
- Resumption
- Extension

Without requiring:
- Summarization
- Reconstruction
- Re-interpretation

## Agency Preservation Invariant

The system must not:
- Compete with human judgment
- Redirect intention
- Optimize engagement
- Collapse agency into preference

## Privacy as Structure

Privacy is enforced structurally by:
- Non-semantic design
- Absence of inference
- Absence of profiling

Privacy is not a policy layer.
