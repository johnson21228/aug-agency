# Commercialization

This document describes the commercialization philosophy of the augmented-agency project.

Commercial activity is not treated as an end in itself, but as a means of sustaining development while preserving core principles.

## Commercialization Goals

Commercialization efforts should:

- Support long-term continuity of the project
- Align incentives with human agency
- Avoid engagement-driven or extractive models
- Respect privacy as a structural requirement

## Non-Goals

The project does not seek to:

- monetize attention
- sell inferred user profiles
- optimize for time-on-platform
- collapse thinking support into productivity metrics

## Acceptable Models

Potential models may include:

- Paid applications or services
- Licensing to organizations aligned with project values
- Professional or individual subscriptions

All models must preserve user control and autonomy.

## Status

This document is directional, not contractual.
Specific commercialization strategies may evolve.
