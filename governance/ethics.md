# Ethics

This document describes the ethical posture of the augmented-agency project.

The project is grounded in the principle that **human agency must be preserved and protected** in digital environments increasingly shaped by autonomous systems.

## Core Ethical Commitments

The system is designed to:

- Preserve human authorship over thought and intention
- Avoid inferring beliefs, goals, or identity
- Avoid manipulating attention or engagement
- Avoid substituting optimization for judgment

Ethical support is achieved structurally, not through policy promises.

## Continuity and Ethics

Continuity is treated as an ethical concern because:

- Fragmentation erodes agency
- Over-inference intrudes into interior thought
- Optimization can redirect intention

By preserving continuity without semantic interpretation, the system supports thinking without competing with it.

## Role of the System

The system does not:
- decide what matters
- rank values
- optimize outcomes
- replace human judgment

It exists to preserve the conditions under which ethical judgment can occur.

## Scope

This document describes ethical intent.
It does not constitute a compliance framework or legal policy.
