# Licensing

This document outlines licensing intent for the augmented-agency project.

Licensing decisions are guided by the goal of:
- enabling adoption
- protecting human agency
- preventing misuse that undermines continuity or autonomy

## Guiding Principles

Licensing should:

- Permit research, exploration, and critique
- Prevent reappropriation into manipulative or extractive systems
- Preserve attribution and architectural integrity

## Separation of Concerns

Different layers of the project may be licensed differently, including:

- Essays and written material
- Ontological and architectural documentation
- Reference implementations
- Commercial applications

Licensing decisions will reflect the role of each layer.

## Status

This document expresses intent only.
Specific licenses are defined in the root `LICENSE` file or future licensing artifacts.
