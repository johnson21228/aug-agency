# Independent Claims (Draft)

1. A computer-implemented system for maintaining longitudinal continuity across time without semantic persistence, the system comprising:
   a numeric continuity substrate configured to store and operate exclusively on numeric continuity data;
   wherein the numeric continuity substrate accepts time-ordered numeric elements supplied by one or more external processes;
   wherein the numeric continuity substrate is structurally incapable of storing, encoding, resolving, or deriving semantic meaning, identity, authorship, or interpretive content from the stored numeric continuity data; and
   wherein the numeric continuity substrate supports continuity operations including persistence across sessions and re-entry into prior continuity regions using only numeric and temporal relationships.

2. A computer-implemented method for maintaining longitudinal continuity across interaction sessions without semantic persistence, the method comprising:
   receiving, from one or more external processes, time-ordered numeric continuity elements corresponding to activity occurring over time;
   persisting the numeric continuity elements in a numeric continuity substrate as numeric continuity data;
   enforcing an architectural invariant that prevents storage, inference, or resolution of semantic meaning within the numeric continuity substrate; and
   enabling re-entry into a prior continuity region by operating on the numeric continuity data using only numeric and temporal relationships.

3. A non-transitory computer-readable medium storing instructions that, when executed by one or more processors, cause the processors to perform the method of claim 2.
