## NUMERIC CONTINUITY SUBSTRATE FOR STRUCTURALLY NON-SEMANTIC
## PERSISTENCE OF LONGITUDINAL CONTINUITY

## FIELD OF THE INVENTION

The present invention relates generally to computational systems and methods for
maintaining longitudinal continuity across digitally mediated processes.
More specifically, the invention relates to a non-semantic, numeric continuity
substrate that enables persistence, re-entry, and continuity operations across
time while being structurally incapable of storing, encoding, resolving, or
deriving semantic meaning, personal data, identity, authorship, or interpretive
content.

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION

1. Structural Conflation of Continuity and Semantics in Prior Art

Existing digital systems implicitly assume that longitudinal continuity requires
the persistence of semantic representations of prior interaction or state. Such
representations may include stored text, embeddings, inferred intent, preferences,
profiles, reconstructed context, or other meaning-bearing artifacts retained
across sessions.

Under this assumption, continuity is achieved only by persisting information
that is interpretable in principle. This conflation of continuity with semantics
is treated as foundational in prior systems.

The present invention rejects this assumption. Semantic persistence is not a
necessary condition for continuity.

2. Inevitable Exploitation Under Semantic Persistence

When continuity is implemented through semantic representations, the retained
state is inherently interpretable, inspectable, and economically extractable.
This creates a persistent control surface for profiling, optimization,
personalization, persuasion, or monetization that arises from the structure of
the representation itself rather than from any particular operator intent.

Accordingly, systems that persist semantic continuity cannot, by design, render
such continuity immune to repurposing or exploitation.

3. Insufficiency of Governance-Based Safeguards

Attempts to mitigate the risks of semantic continuity through encryption, access
control, anonymization, minimization, or consent mechanisms do not eliminate the
underlying structural property: semantic meaning is retained and may be resolved
in principle.

As a result, governance-based or policy-based safeguards are insufficient to make
semantic continuity structurally safe.

4. Continuity as a Required System Function

Independent of how interaction is presented, modern computational systems
increasingly require continuity across time. Such continuity includes, without
limitation, the ability to resume prior activity, re-enter ongoing processes,
coordinate across sessions, maintain temporal relationships, and preserve
longitudinal structure.

As interaction is increasingly mediated through conversational, assistive, or
agent-based interfaces, continuity becomes a prerequisite for human agency rather
than a convenience. Without structural continuity, human interaction collapses
into episodic prompts and responses, eroding authorship, directionality, and the
ability to carry intention forward over time, even in the presence of highly
capable automation.

5. Need for a Non-Semantic Continuity Primitive

There exists a need for a computational primitive that provides longitudinal
continuity while being structurally incapable of storing, resolving, or exposing
semantic meaning, identity, personal information, or interpretive content.

Such a primitive must provide continuity as a system resource while eliminating
the interpretability and economic extractability inherent in semantic memory
persistence.

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION

The present invention provides a numeric continuity substrate that persists
longitudinal continuity across interaction sessions while being structurally
incapable of encoding semantic meaning, personal data, identity, authorship, or
interpretive content.

Continuity within the invention is represented exclusively as time-ordered numeric
structures (“traces”) supplied by one or more external processes. The substrate
does not interpret, infer, classify, or assign meaning to these structures and
operates solely on numeric continuity coordinates and temporal relationships.

The continuity substrate may serve as a foundational component of an Intelligent
Augmented Memory (IAM) system, in which computation does not infer or replace human
judgment, but instead preserves the structural conditions under which human
thinking can persist, be resumed, and evolve across time.

Traces may originate from any external source, including without limitation
human-generated interactions, automated agents, tools, sensors, or system
processes. Human-generated traces represent a non-exclusive subclass and are not
structurally privileged within the substrate.

### The invention enables continuity operations including, without limitation
persistence across sessions, re-entry into prior continuity regions, recurrence
detection, drift or divergence measurement, and stability or fragmentation
analysis.

By rendering continuity state non-semantic and non-interpretable, the invention
transforms continuity from an exploitable memory asset into a bounded
computational resource whose structure may be reused without exposing or
retaining meaning, independent of operator intent, incentive, or policy.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION

1. Overview

The invention defines a foundational continuity layer that may be incorporated
into higher-level systems while remaining strictly non-semantic. The layer
persists continuity structure across time while enforcing an invariant that
semantic meaning cannot be stored, inferred, or resolved within the substrate.

The invention operates independently of any particular application, interface,
domain, or inference model.

2. Numeric Continuity Substrate (NCS)

The Numeric Continuity Substrate (NCS) comprises a storage and computation layer
configured to accept and persist discrete continuity elements (“continuons”).
### Each continuon consists of
- a numeric continuity coordinate set,
- a temporal position or ordering index, and
- an opaque numeric identifier.

All values persisted within the substrate are numeric and structurally incapable
of encoding semantic meaning.

3. Trace Definition and Source Agnosticism

A trace is defined as a time-ordered numeric sequence supplied to the substrate
by an external process. The substrate does not encode, infer, or retain the
source, intent, authorship, or interpretation of any trace.

Human-generated traces, agent-generated traces, and system-generated traces are
structurally indistinguishable within the substrate.

4. Structural Invariants

### The substrate enforces the following invariants
- no semantic content is stored;
- no persisted value is resolvable to meaning, identity, authorship, or personal
  data within the substrate;
- no interpretation, classification, or inference is performed on stored values.

These invariants are enforced at the architectural level and do not depend on
policy, configuration, operator discretion, or deployment context.

5. Separation from Semantic Systems

Semantic interpretation, language processing, or meaning assignment may occur, if
at all, in systems external to the substrate. Any such processing is ephemeral
and does not alter, enrich, annotate, or persist within the continuity state
stored by the substrate.

Associations between continuity identifiers and semantic content may exist in
external systems but are not accessible to, nor resolvable by, the substrate
itself.

6. Continuity Access Protocol (CAP)

Access to the substrate is mediated by a Continuity Access Protocol (CAP) that
### exposes only structural operations over continuity state. CAP permits
- insertion of numeric continuity data,
- retrieval of continuity neighborhoods,
- traversal of continuity chains, and
- detection of recurrence or re-entry signals.

CAP does not expose semantic joins, content resolution, identity lookup, or
interpretive operations.

7. Continuity Operations Without Meaning

### Using only numeric continuity data, the substrate supports computation of
- persistence across sessions,
- recurrence of prior continuity regions,
- drift or divergence over time,
- stability or fragmentation of continuity segments, and
- re-entry into prior continuity neighborhoods.

Re-entry enables a human or external system to resume an ongoing cognitive or
procedural process without reconstructing context or re-establishing intent.

## METHOD OF MAINTAINING LONGITUDINAL CONTINUITY

A method for maintaining longitudinal continuity across interaction sessions
### without semantic persistence, the method comprising

(a) receiving, from one or more external processes, a sequence of numeric
continuity elements corresponding to activity occurring over time;

(b) persisting the numeric continuity elements within a continuity substrate as
time-ordered continuity state while structurally preventing storage of semantic
meaning, identity, authorship, intent, or interpretive content;

(c) enforcing an architectural invariant that prohibits semantic interpretation,
inference, classification, or prediction over the persisted continuity state;

(d) enabling re-entry into a prior continuity region by traversing the persisted
numeric continuity state using only numeric and temporal relationships; and

(e) providing continuity signals sufficient to resume an ongoing process without
reconstructing semantic context or re-establishing inferred intent.

The method may further comprise detecting recurrence of a prior continuity region
based solely on numeric proximity, temporal alignment, or continuity coordinate
relationships.

The method may further comprise computing drift or divergence between continuity
regions using numeric continuity coordinates without semantic comparison.

The method may further comprise identifying fragmentation of longitudinal
continuity based on discontinuities in numeric continuity sequences.

The method may further comprise enabling a human to resume a partially completed
cognitive or procedural process without re-articulating goals, prompts, or
intent.

The method may further comprise receiving numeric continuity elements from a
plurality of sources including human interaction, automated agents, tools, or
system processes, without structural differentiation within the continuity
substrate.

The method may further comprise persisting continuity state on a user-controlled
device without reliance on centralized semantic processing.

The method explicitly excludes generating summaries, inferred representations,
embeddings, semantic compressions, or semantic substitutes for prior activity.

The method further excludes using continuity state to predict behavior, optimize
outcomes, infer preferences, or construct user profiles.

8. Structural Impossibility of Exploitation

Because the substrate is structurally incapable of resolving continuity
structures to meaning, no system operator, inference model, or downstream process
can extract behavioral profiles, preferences, persuasive leverage, or semantic
content from continuity state alone.

This property holds regardless of deployment environment, incentive structure, or
governance framework.

9. Deployment Characteristics

The invention does not require centralized inference infrastructure or cloud-
based semantic processing. Continuity may be persisted locally, including on
user-controlled devices, without reliance on large-scale inference systems.

Semantic processing, if performed, must remain ephemeral and non-persistent with
respect to the continuity substrate.

10. Role as an Enabling Primitive

The Numeric Continuity Substrate constitutes an enabling architectural primitive
upon which higher-level continuity-aware and agency-preserving systems may be
constructed, including Intelligent Augmented Memory (IAM) systems. Such systems
may provide task resumption, multi-session coordination, or longitudinal
cognitive support without inheriting the exploitability associated with semantic
memory persistence.

## ADVANTAGES OF THE INVENTION

### The invention provides, among other advantages
- persistence of continuity without semantic storage;
- structural immunity to profiling and manipulation;
- independence from operator trust or policy assurances;
- compatibility with on-device and privacy-preserving systems; and
- suitability as a foundational layer for continuity-dependent and agency-
  preserving applications.

## CONCLUSION

### The invention introduces a previously unavailable computational primitive
persistent longitudinal continuity that is structurally incapable of encoding
meaning. By decoupling continuity from semantics at the architectural level, the
invention constrains future systems to treat semantic persistence as optional
rather than inherent, thereby preserving human agency and eliminating the
exploitability intrinsic to prior art continuity mechanisms.
