# Summary

The disclosed system introduces a continuity-first architecture for supporting human agency in digital environments.

The system captures explicit human language inputs as non-semantic traces, preserving temporal ordering and structural relationships without interpreting meaning or inferring intent. These traces are organized into continuity structures that enable re-entry into prior activity without summarization or reconstruction.

In some embodiments, the system is exposed through a conversational interface that allows users to generate, revisit, and extend thought over time. The system does not generate conclusions or optimize outcomes; instead, it preserves the conditions under which human imagination, attention, and meaning-making can occur.

Privacy is enforced structurally through the absence of semantic inference, rather than through policy-based controls. Agents may interact with the system only through explicit instruction and may not infer goals or authorship.

By separating continuity from semantics and execution, the disclosed system enables human autonomy to persist alongside increasingly capable agentic systems.
