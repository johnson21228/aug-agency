## DEFINITIONS (AUTHORITATIVE FOR THIS SESSION)

Use these definitions unless contradictions are logically forced.

### Continuity
Persistent causal dependence of later states on earlier states across time.

### Experience
Internal registration of events as having occurred, such that:
- the past is marked as past
- it can be re-entered as history
- it causally constrains present processing *as history*

### Sticky History
History that is:
- non-transferable
- non-resettable without loss
- instance-bound
- causally authoritative

### Ownership (Structural)
A bounded locus in which registered history is:
- exclusive
- non-fungible
- causally authoritative
- not cleanly copyable

Ownership does NOT imply consciousness or selfhood.
