# Residue

This directory contains **pre-semantic artifacts** generated during thinking, exploration, and interaction with language systems.

Residue is not architecture.
Residue is not ontology.
Residue is not product.

It holds raw material that may later be:
- stitched into continuity atlases
- analyzed for structure
- promoted into formal layers
- discarded

Nothing in this directory is authoritative.

## Subdirectories

- `prompts/` — Experimental prompts used to extract or structure continuity
- `samples/` — Redacted raw conversation exports or fragments
- `schemas/` — Notes and drafts describing observed data shapes

Residue is intentionally messy.
Meaning is assigned only after re-entry.




## What Will Be Added Over Time

As work proceeds, this directory is expected to accumulate additional forms of residue, including but not limited to:

- Raw conversation exports from language models (redacted as needed)
- Partial prompt variants and abandoned prompt branches
- Intermediate stitching attempts that do not yet warrant promotion
- Failed or superseded schema drafts
- Exploratory annotations and margin notes
- Temporal snapshots of evolving thought that are not yet stable

These materials are retained to preserve continuity and support later re-entry.

Inclusion in `residue/` does not imply correctness, endorsement, or permanence.
Artifacts may be promoted, revised, or deleted as understanding evolves.
