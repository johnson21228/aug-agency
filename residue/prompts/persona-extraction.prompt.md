# Persona Extraction Prompt (Residue)

Purpose:
This prompt explores whether and how personas appear to emerge from
longitudinal interaction traces when viewed structurally.

Scope:
- Exploratory only
- Used to observe patterns, not to assert identity
- Used to test limits of non-semantic handling

Constraints:
- Personas are not ground truth
- No identity claims are made
- No persistence is assumed

Notes:
This prompt is intentionally risky and must remain in residue.
Any outputs must be treated as hypotheses, not facts.

Promotion:
If structural persona patterns are consistently observed, they may later
be discussed at the architectural or ethical level.
