# Translation Hints (Non-Authoritative)

## Scope and Authority

This file provides non-authoritative translation guidance derived from
human review and correction.

English source texts remain canonical.

This file:
- Supplements the canonical translation prompt
- Does NOT override the source text
- Does NOT authorize reinterpretation, paraphrase, or stylistic improvement
- Exists to prevent known failure modes in translation

This document records recurring issues, corrections, and preferences
identified through human-in-the-loop review. It is intended to constrain
error, not to redefine meaning.

---

## Usage Guidance

When translating:

1. Apply the canonical translation prompt first.
2. Consult this file only when:
   - Choosing between multiple valid translations
   - Reviewing drafts for conceptual drift or distortion
3. If tension exists between fluency and these hints:
   - Prefer fidelity to meaning, structure, and continuity.

This file must not be treated as a glossary or as an authoritative mapping.
It exists to preserve intent, not to optimize language.

---

## Terminology (Known Failure Modes)

- **"continuity"**
  - MUST NOT be translated as optimization, efficiency, or performance
  - Refers to temporal persistence of thought, meaning, and re-engagement

- **"re-entry"**
  - MUST preserve a temporal / experiential sense
  - MUST NOT be rendered as UI navigation, retrieval, or browsing metaphor

- **"agency"**
  - MUST NOT collapse into economic, managerial, or incentive-only framing
  - Refers to human authorship, judgment, and intention over time

---

## Tone and Structure

- Preserve philosophical seriousness and analytical restraint.
- Avoid motivational, instructional, or promotional tone.
- Do not simplify argumentative structure for readability.
- Maintain sentence length and cumulative reasoning where it carries meaning.
- Do not add emphasis, examples, or clarification not present in the source.

---

## Swiss German Notes (When Applicable)

- Prefer Alemannic phrasing over High German where possible.
- Avoid legalistic or bureaucratic constructions.
- This is reader-facing philosophical prose, not technical or regulatory text.
