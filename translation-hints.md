# Translation Hints (Non-Authoritative)

This document records translation preferences and recurring corrections
identified through human review.

English remains canonical.
This document does not override the translation prompt.

## Terminology

- "continuity" → do NOT translate as optimization or efficiency
- "re-entry" → preserve temporal / experiential sense, not navigation metaphor
- "agency" → avoid economic-only connotations

## Tone

- Preserve philosophical seriousness.
- Avoid motivational or instructional tone.
- Do not simplify argumentative structure for readability.

## Swiss German Notes

- Prefer Alemannic phrasing over High German where possible.
- Avoid legalistic constructions; this is reader-facing.
