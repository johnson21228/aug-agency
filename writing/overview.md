# Overview

This document states the structural premises of the repository.
It does not argue for them.
The essays that follow explore their consequences.

Continuity preserves a single human’s personal language use as a path through time. When that path is embedded in semantic space, it acquires global shape. The substrate holds this shape independently of interpretation, while meaning is given by the human through their choice of coordinates and metric. A continuity map exposes this structure so the human can re-enter, navigate, and remain in time with their own thinking rather than reconstructing it from artifacts.
